Privacy Policy

Twofold · Last updated 9 August 2026

Twofold is a tool people use while sitting across from a client. Your documents, your notes and your clients' signatures stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to us, because there is no “us” to upload them to — Twofold has no server.

What stays on your device

All of it. Specifically:

  • Documents you import. A copy is saved in Twofold's private app storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Your private notes and talk track. Stored alongside the document, and kept in a separate file so that sharing a document can never accidentally include them.
  • Signatures and signed copies. The signature is drawn on your device and flattened into a PDF stored on your device.
  • Your meeting log. Which document was shown, when, to the label you typed, and whether it was signed.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Twofold works with no signal at all, which is deliberate — it is designed to be used in living rooms and bank branches where there is no connection.

What leaves your device

One thing: your subscription status.

If you buy Twofold Pro, the purchase is handled by the Galaxy Store and validated through RevenueCat, which is the billing service Twofold uses. To do that, RevenueCat receives:

DataWhy
A random anonymous ID generated on your device To remember that this install has an active subscription. It is not linked to your name, email or Samsung account.
Purchase receipt and subscription status To confirm the purchase is genuine and still active
Device model, OS version, app version, country Sent by the billing SDK to process and troubleshoot purchases

Payment itself is handled entirely by Samsung. Twofold never sees your card details. We do not receive your name or email address, and we do not ask for an account — there is no sign-up.

RevenueCat's handling of this data is covered by their own privacy policy, and the Galaxy Store purchase by Samsung's.

What Twofold does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address collected.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected to sell.

Permissions

Twofold requests no runtime permissions at all. It never asks for your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

The app declares three install-time permissions, all for billing:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only to validate a subscription. Nothing else in the app makes a network request.
  • The Samsung in-app purchase permission — used to complete a purchase in the Galaxy Store.

When you import a document, Android's own file picker asks you to choose a single file, and Twofold receives access to that file only — not to your storage.

Children

Twofold is a professional tool for adults at work. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your documents, notes, signed copies and meeting log stay on your device until you remove them. Deleting a document removes it from Twofold. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, permanently.

Because your content never reaches a server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us to have it erased.

To delete the subscription record held by RevenueCat, or the purchase record held by Samsung, contact them or us using the details below and we will pass the request on.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. Since Twofold holds no personal data about you, most of these have nothing to act on — but if you believe otherwise, write to us and we will answer.

Changes

If Twofold ever starts collecting something it does not collect today, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
